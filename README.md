# E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard analyzes customer behavior, order patterns, product performance, revenue trends, and customer segmentation to generate actionable business insights.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and order performance
- Identify top-performing products and departments
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Analyze reorder and retention patterns
- Identify peak ordering hours and days
- Segment customers based on revenue contribution
- Identify factors associated with higher reorder rates
- Drill down into major revenue drivers

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 — E-Commerce Pulse

Focuses on overall business performance and customer value.

**Key Analysis:**
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Items per Order
- Reorder Rate
- Monthly Revenue Growth
- Top 10 Products by Revenue
- Revenue by Department
- Customer Value Analysis
- Customer Revenue Segmentation
- Weekday vs Weekend Orders

### Page 2 — Customer & Order Insights

Focuses on deeper customer and order behavior.

**Key Analysis:**
- Orders by Hour
- Orders by Day of Week
- Revenue by Day of Week
- Top 10 Products by Orders
- Reorder Rate by Customer Segment
- Revenue Contribution by Customer Segment
- Customer Frequency vs Revenue
- Customer Segment Performance
- Key Influencers Analysis
- Revenue Drivers using Decomposition Tree

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- When do customers place the most orders?
- Which products generate the highest revenue and order volume?
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- What factors are associated with higher reorder rates?
